Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy Last updated: November 7, 2026 This policy covers the Japanese Daily apps ("the App") for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and for Android. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum. Where iOS and Android differ, the sections below say so. Information we collect • We do not require an account and do not collect your name or contacts. • Your learning progress (stars, kana results, bookmarks, streak) and settings are stored on your device. On iOS they also sync through your own iCloud account, which we cannot read. On Android they stay on your device only: the App does not use Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer. • Analytics and crash reports: we use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes. This includes anonymous usage events (for example which screens are opened and challenge results), crash logs, and device information such as model, operating system version and language. Firebase assigns an installation identifier to each install of the App; we do not link it to you. • Advertising: the free version shows ads via Google AdMob. Ads are always non-personalized: we do not use your advertising identifier to personalize ads, and on iOS the App does not ask to track you. Google may still use device and ad identifiers, your IP address (which gives an approximate location) and ad interactions to deliver ads, limit how often they repeat, measure them and prevent fraud. In regions that require it, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent form is shown first. • Surveys and feedback (optional): if you complete the first-launch questions, rate the App, or send feedback, we store your answers on our server. These rows hold what you chose to send (your answers, a rating, your message, and an email address only if you type one) plus device and app details: app version, operating system, device model, language and region, text size, appearance, the screen you were on, whether you have Premium, and counts such as challenges passed. They carry no user ID, advertising ID or other identifier, so we cannot tell which rows came from the same person. • Purchases are handled by Apple on iOS and by Google Play on Android. We receive your purchase status, not your payment details. Whether you have Premium, and which plan, is included in analytics and survey data as described above. How we use information • To operate and improve the App, diagnose problems, read your feedback, and show ads in the free version. Third parties • Google (Firebase, AdMob and Google Play) and Apple (App Store, in-app purchases, iCloud) process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your data. • Survey and feedback rows are stored on our own server (cms.wahthefox.com), over an encrypted connection. Your choices • Ads are non-personalized for everyone. Where a consent form is shown, you can decline it. • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads). • Purchasing Premium removes ads. • Surveys, ratings and feedback are optional. Leave the email field empty if you do not want a reply. To have a feedback row that contains your email address deleted, send us feedback that quotes that address. Data retention and security • On-device data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the App. On iOS, synced progress also lives in your iCloud until you delete it there. • Data sent over the network is encrypted in transit. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google according to Firebase's retention settings. Survey rows are kept until we delete them. • Because we hold no account or identifier for you, we cannot look up your data by name. Children • The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Changes • We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change. Contact • Questions? Use the Feedback option in Settings to reach us.
