Privacy Policy Last updated: August 6, 2026 This app ("the App") helps you study Japanese. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum. This policy explains what we collect and how it is used. Ads may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to be more relevant, only if you allow tracking when prompted. • Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive your purchase status but not your payment details. How we use information • To operate and improve the App, diagnose problems, and show ads in the free version. Third parties • Google (Firebase, AdMob) and Apple (App Store, in-app purchases) process data under their own privacy policies. Your choices • You can decline app tracking when prompted; ads will then be non-personalized. • Purchasing Premium removes ads. Data retention and security • On-device data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the App. Children • The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Changes • We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change. Contact • Questions? Use the Feedback option in Settings to reach us.
