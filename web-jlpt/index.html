Pick your level and the lesson list follows it. Each level below shows its true share of the climb — and a first taste of its words.

Then sit the test in miniature

Every lesson ends in a ladder of challenges: ten questions a rung, 80% to pass, three stars for a perfect run. The rungs harden as you climb — first you recognise the word, then you recall it, and near the top you only hear it, spoken aloud with nothing on screen. Pass a rung and the next one opens.

Sweep the opening lessons — three stars on every challenge — and the whole of N5 unlocks for good, free.
